## Dataset
This analysis used data from the General Social Survey (GSS) 2018 dataset. The project examined how demographic and socioeconomic factors, including race, sex, education level, quality of life, and federal income tax, could be used to predict marital status through decision tree classification.

Source:
https://gss.norc.org/

## Key Findings
- Race was identified as the strongest predictor of marital status.
- Quality of life and sex provided additional predictive value within the decision tree model.
- The model successfully segmented respondents into distinct groups with different marital status distributions.
- Terminal Node 6, consisting of White female respondents reporting an excellent or very good quality of life, showed similar marital status distributions in both the Training and Test samples.
- Training and Test sample comparisons indicated consistent classification patterns and limited evidence of overfitting.
- Results suggested good model stability and reasonable generalization to unseen data.
